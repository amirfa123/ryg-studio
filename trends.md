## Video ideas for this week (all new; none in topics.md)

Format: question — category (#topic) — science hook. Verify any non-obvious number with a quick search before scripting.

### Pop culture / trending
1. ✅ **Could a dog really rescue you at sea like in GTA 6?** — pop culture + GTA 6 (#gta6)
   - Real lifeguard dogs (big water breeds like Newfoundlands) are trained to tow swimmers to shore.
   - In Italy they even jump from helicopters.
   - Stand-in: a generic dog with shades.
2. ✅ **What would it take for a scooter to fly like the GTA 6 glitch?** — pop culture + GTA 6 (#gta6)
   - Lift needs speed and wing area.
   - To lift ~150 kg at 50 km/h, you'd need a wing roughly the size of a hang glider (~12 m²).
3. **How long is 125 million hours (the time people spent watching GTA 6 streams in one day)?** — pop culture + GTA 6 (#gta6)
   - That's about 14,000 years of non-stop watching, back to the end of the last Ice Age.
4. ✅ **Do snakes really unhinge their jaws, like the GTA 6 pet snake?** — pop culture + GTA 6 / animals (#gta6)
   - They don't unhinge them.
   - The two halves of the lower jaw aren't fused, so they stretch apart on a stretchy ligament and "walk" the prey in.
5. ✅ **How strong is an alligator's bite (the thing eating everyone in GTA 6)?** — pop culture + GTA 6 / animals (#gta6)
   - Around 2,000+ psi, one of the strongest bites measured.
   - But the muscles that OPEN the jaw are so weak that a rubber band can hold it shut.
6. **How far could you really shoot an arrow in the Hunger Games?** — pop culture (#hungergames)
   - A modern bow fires an arrow at ~90–100 m/s.
   - Olympic targets are at 70 m, but the flight record (with a foot bow) is over 1.8 km.
7. ✅ **What would happen if you trained in 100× gravity like in Dragon Ball?** — pop culture (#dragonball)
   - A 70 kg person would weigh 7 tonnes.
   - Your heart couldn't pump blood up to your brain; untrained people black out at around 5 g.
   - Stand-in: generic spiky-haired fighter, no copied designs.
8. **How tall do you have to be to dunk without jumping?** — pop culture (#nba, rides Wemby's 50)
   - The rim is 3.05 m.
   - A 2.24 m player's standing reach is close to 3 m, so he's almost there flat-footed. Verify the reach.
   - Unnamed player, plain jersey.
9. **How do you catch a falling rocket with giant chopsticks?** — pop culture / space (#starship)
   - The ship falls from orbit at ~27,000 km/h.
   - The air does most of the braking, then the engines slow it to near-hover right beside the tower.
   - Generic rocket and tower, no logos.

### Body / everyday
10. ✅ **Why do your eyes hurt after gaming all day?** — body + GTA 6 tie-in (#eyes)
    - When you stare at a screen, your blinking drops by about half, so your eyes dry out.
    - Focusing at one distance for hours also tires the focusing muscles.
11. ✅ **Why do songs get stuck in your head?** — body (#earworm, rides the Mariah Christmas takeover)
    - Over 90% of people get an "earworm" at least weekly.
    - Your brain replays unfinished, catchy loops.
12. ✅ **Why do you always have room for dessert?** — body (#thanksgiving)
    - "Sensory-specific satiety": you get bored of one flavor, but a new taste resets your appetite.
13. ✅ **Why does your nose run when it's cold?** — body (#cold)
    - Your nose warms and wets cold, dry air.
    - It overproduces mucus, and warm breath condenses at the tip.
14. ✅ **Why do you get butterflies in your stomach?** — body (#butterflies, rides "My Moon My Man")
    - Adrenaline pulls blood away from your gut toward your muscles.
    - Your stomach "flutters" and slows down.

### Crazy "what would happen if…"
15. ✅ **What would happen if you went to space without a spacesuit?** — what-if (#space)
    - You'd stay conscious for about 10–15 s.
    - You wouldn't explode, but your saliva would boil and you'd swell.
    - The "could YOU" angle helps space do better.
16. ✅ **What would happen if Earth had rings like Saturn?** — what-if (#saturn)
    - They'd arc across the sky as a giant bright band.
    - Their shadow would make some places noticeably colder in winter.
17. ✅ **What would happen if the Moon crashed into Earth?** — what-if (#moon)
    - It would break apart before touching us (the Roche limit, ~18,000 km).
    - Earth would get a ring, then a rain of molten rock.

### Wildcard
18. ✅ **Can a turkey actually fly?** — wildcard/animals (#turkey, Thanksgiving week)
    - Wild turkeys can burst-fly at around 80–90 km/h.
    - Farm turkeys are bred too heavy to get off the ground.
19. **Why can you see your breath in winter?** — wildcard/everyday (#winter)
    - Your breath is full of warm water vapor.
    - Cold air makes it condense into a tiny cloud, the same way clouds form.
20. ✅ **How much would it cost to buy everything on Black Friday?** — wildcard/money (#blackfriday)
    - US shoppers spend around $10B+ online in a single day. Verify the 2026 figure.
    - Cash-stack comparison against buildings.
    - Keep it light, no store logos.
21. **Why do cats knead you with their paws?** — wildcard/animals (#cat, the cats short did well on FB)
    - It's leftover kitten behavior from nursing.
    - Scent glands in their paws mark you as "theirs".
