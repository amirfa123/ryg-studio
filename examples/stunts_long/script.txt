Could you survive real-life GTA stunts? In the game, you launch a car off a ramp, fall off a roof, dive off a skyscraper... and just get back up. GTA 6 drops today, so let's test it. Six famous stunts. Real physics. One round yellow guy. And at the end, we count how many he actually survives. Some of these, people have really done. Some would end you instantly. Let's find out which is which.

Stunt one: the ramp jump. In GTA you hit a ramp, fly over half the city, and drive away. So how far can a real car fly? Take a guess... In 2011, a stunt driver launched a truck about one hundred meters. A whole football field! But the flying part is easy. The landing is what gets you. Land on flat ground and all that falling speed stops in an instant. Real stunt drivers land on a second ramp that slopes away, so they touch down gently. Verdict: survivable... but only with a perfect landing ramp.

Stunt two: jumping off a roof. In the game you drop three floors and lose a tiny bit of health. In real life? Here's the scary number. Doctors say a fall of about four stories, around fifteen meters, is deadly about half the time. You'd hit the ground at roughly sixty kilometers per hour. Even one or two floors can break your legs. Your body has to stop in a split second, and the shorter that stop, the bigger the force. Free runners survive drops by landing on bent legs and rolling to spread the impact. So, one floor, maybe. Four floors? Not a chance.

Stunt three: jumping out of a moving car. GTA makes it look like a cool little tumble. But think about it... At one hundred kilometers per hour, your body keeps going at that speed after you leave the seat. Hitting the road like that is like falling off a thirteen-story building, plus sliding across rough asphalt. Movie stunt doubles do this at a crawl, in padding, onto soft mats. Verdict: no way.

Stunt four: the endless wheelie. In GTA you can pop a wheelie and ride it down the whole highway. Guess how far a real rider has gone on one wheel... Three hundred and thirty-one kilometers, non-stop! That's a world record from 1991. The wheelie itself isn't the danger. Balance is. Lean back too far, and the bike flips right on top of you. So this one? You'd survive... if you trained for years.

Stunt five: diving off a skyscraper into the ocean. Water is soft, right? Here's the twist. Water can't squish. It has to get out of your way, and at high speed, it can't move fast enough. The highest cliff jump ever was about fifty-nine meters, hitting the water at about one hundred and twenty kilometers per hour. A skyscraper is three or four times taller. Hit the water from there, and it feels a lot like concrete. That's why pro divers go in feet first, toes pointed, like a pencil, to slice into the water. Verdict: nope.

Stunt six: falling from a plane with no parachute. Surely this is impossible... right? Well, in 2016, a skydiver jumped from seven thousand six hundred meters with no parachute at all. He fell for about two minutes, at around one hundred and ninety kilometers per hour, and landed in a giant net, thirty meters wide. And he walked away! But without that net? There's no surviving it.

Bonus round: sprinting forever. GTA characters sprint across the entire map. Real humans? Even the fastest sprinter ever, at about forty-four kilometers per hour, can only hold top speed for a few seconds. Your muscles' instant fuel runs out in about ten seconds. After that, you slow down, no matter what. That's why sprinters are actually slowing down at the end of a hundred-meter race. The winner is just the one who slows down the least.

So, the final score. Out of six stunts, Round Yellow Guy survives... two. The ramp jump, with a perfect landing, and the wheelie, with years of practice. Everything else? Game over. And in real life, there's no respawn button at the hospital. So enjoy GTA 6... and leave the stunts to the game.
