How do you get rich fast in GTA 6? The game has been out for two days, and everyone is asking the same thing: how do I make my first million? So we dug through the early player guides, crunched the numbers, and built the fastest route from broke to rich. Seven steps, from your very first dollar to the biggest payday in the game. One round yellow guy. And one question: how fast can you actually hit a million? Let's get that money.

Step one: don't lose what you already have. Here's something a lot of players miss. The cash in your pocket has a limit, about twenty thousand dollars. And if you get wasted or busted... take a guess how much of it you keep. Zero. All of it, gone. So the first rule of getting rich is boring, but huge: every time you make a big score, go straight to an ATM and bank it.

Step two: cars. Early on, the simplest steady money is stealing cars and selling them to a guy named Moses. Older, low-key cars usually get you about a thousand dollars each, and you can do it again every couple of minutes. Sounds great... until you do the math. To reach one million dollars at a thousand per car, you'd need... about one thousand cars. That's a lot of driving. So cars are a starter, not the plan. They keep your pockets full while you unlock the good stuff.

Step three: racing. Here's a way to earn without anyone chasing you. Win a demolition derby or a race, and you can take home roughly four to six thousand dollars a win. Smash, crash, collect. Renting a car to race only costs a few hundred, so almost every win is pure profit. And winning races also counts toward completing the whole game. Fun, safe-ish, and it adds up.

Step four: the big upgrade. Later on, things get serious. Jewelry stores can pay about ten to twenty-five thousand dollars a job. And a car collector named Randy has a wish list of eighteen fancy cars, often worth thirty thousand dollars or more each. Guess what the whole list adds up to... more than half a million dollars! Same idea as step two, just way shinier cars.

Step five: become a delivery pilot. A pilot named Pete hires you to fly drops, and each one can pay about twelve to twenty thousand dollars if every package lands before the timer runs out. It's fast, it's fun, and you get to fly. One good run pays as much as a dozen cars for Moses.

Step six: the jackpot. So what's the single biggest payday? Bank vaults. Once you unlock them near the end of chapter four, one vault can pay up to about two hundred thousand dollars. And once you know the route, guides say a run can take around five minutes. Five vaults... and you're a millionaire. But there's a catch. The second you grab that money, you get four police stars. Instantly. And remember step one... get busted with that loot, and it's gone.

Bonus round: what NOT to waste time on. Mugging? A couple hundred bucks. Barely worth it. Towing away ATMs? Slow, loud, and the cops notice. And treasure diving underwater? Guides call it extremely time consuming. Skip these if you want speed.

Step seven: the savings trick. The game has a family savings account. Put in five thousand dollars, and your bank limit goes up. Put in more, and you unlock bigger limits and discounts. But here's the twist: deposits can never come back out. It's money you lock away for good. The final goal in there? Two million dollars.

So, how do you get rich fast in GTA 6? Start with cars and races. Bank every score. Level up to jewelry stores, supercars and pilot runs. Then hit the vaults. Do that, and your first million is just a few vault runs away. Just remember... this is all in the game. In real life, the fastest way to get rich is still... a lot slower. Good luck out there.
